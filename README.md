E-commerce Sales Performance Dashboard

📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Subha Dey's Ecommerce Sales Report. It is designed to track, visualize, and analyze key performance indicators (KPIs) such as total sales amount, profit, quantity sold, and average order value (AOV). The interactive dynamic filters allow users to drill down into the data by specific quarters and states to uncover actionable business insights.

📊 Key Features & Visualizations
KPI Cards: Quick insights into major business metrics:

Total Sales (Sum of Amount): 438K

Total Profit: 37K

Total Quantity Sold: 6K

Sum of AOV (Average Order Value): 121.014K

Profitability Analysis (Profit by Month): A monthly breakdown showing profitable periods (peaks in January, February, and November) versus months with negative margins (May, July, December).

Geographical Performance: A horizontal bar chart displaying the Sum of Amount by State, highlighting top-performing regions like Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and Uttar Pradesh.

Customer Insights: Identifies high-value customers (e.g., Harivansh, Madhav, Madan Mohan) through the Sum of Amount by CustomerName chart.

Product Performance: * Category Breakdown: A donut chart showing sales share by category, dominated heavily by Clothing (62.62%), followed by Electronics (20.55%) and Furniture (16.83%).

Top Sub-Categories: A detailed horizontal bar chart showing the most profitable sub-categories, led by Printers, Bookcases, and Saree.

Payment Method Preferences: A donut chart illustrating consumer behavior, showing that Cash on Delivery (COD) is the most preferred method (43.74%), followed closely by UPI (20.61%).

🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
Power BI Desktop: For data modeling, DAX calculations, and dashboard creation.

Data Source: E-commerce sales data (Excel/CSV dataset containing order details).

💡 Key Insights from the Dashboard
Clothing is King: More than 62% of the total quantities sold belong to the Clothing category, making it the primary volume driver.



Profitability Drivers: Even though clothing has the highest quantity share, specific sub-categories like Printers and Bookcases contribute heavily to the overall profit.

Payment Trends: Traditional payment methods like COD still dominate nearly half of the market share, though digital modes (UPI & Cards) combine for a significant portion.
